What Happens After You Contact Us
1. We Understand Your Enquiry
We review your summary to identify the type of matter and any current deadline, and request what is needed for a clearer understanding. Identifying the parties, key document and previous procedure helps direct the enquiry to the appropriate specialist.
2. We Explain the Service and Documents Needed
We identify with you the documents required, scope of advice or work, and fees. Requirements may differ if the matter needs translation, technical expertise or representation by a licensed advocate.
3. We Agree the Appointment or Engagement
We coordinate the means of communication and an available appointment where needed, and begin work once the engagement is accepted and its scope agreed. Initial contact and document exchange can take place remotely, with any later attendance, power of attorney or authentication requirements explained.

Questions Before Contacting Us
Do I need to have an ongoing case?
No. You can contact us to review a contract before signing, structure a partnership, discuss a claim, or address a family or regulatory matter, as well as matters where procedures have already begun.
How are consultation fees determined?
They depend on the nature of the matter, volume of documents and type of review required. We explain the service scope and fees before agreeing to start work. External charges or services may be defined separately where needed.
Can I make initial contact from outside the UAE?
Initial contact and document exchange can take place remotely. Any later requirements concerning attendance, a power of attorney, authentication or translation are explained according to the matter, authority and procedure.
Does an acknowledgement of receipt confirm my appointment?
An acknowledgement confirms that the request has reached the system. The consultation appointment, acceptance of the engagement and scope of work are determined through communication and agreement. Monitoring of legal deadlines does not begin merely because the form was submitted.
Can I send an urgent matter outside office hours?
You can send a WhatsApp message identifying the relevant event or deadline and its date. Replies are subject to availability. Do not rely on sending alone as confirmation that follow-up has been accepted or a deadline protected.
