What it can show
When the cheque became payable, and whether it was dated in advance.

Payee
What it can show

Amount in words
What it can show
The amount written out in full by the person who signed.
What we check
We compare the words with the figures and note any difference for review.
Amount in figures
What it can show
The amount written in numbers in the amount box.
What we check
We check it against the amount in words, the return memo and the invoice or agreement it relates to.
Signature
What it can show
Who signed for the account, and whether the account holder is a person or a company.
What we check
We note who signed and in what capacity, and whether the memo mentions a signature issue.
Return memo
What it can show
The reason the bank gave for not paying the cheque, and when it was returned.
What we check
We read the stated reason and date closely, because different reasons can lead to different next steps.

What a Review of Your Matter Can Clarify
Many legal problems begin before a complaint or court claim is filed: when a contract is signed, a settlement accepted, goods delivered without a record or a partnership formed without defining rights. An early review helps you understand the implications of a decision before they become difficult to address.
We explain what the documents support and what needs further evidence, then compare the available options in terms of viability, cost and risk. Depending on the stage your matter has reached, the appropriate next step may be negotiation, a claim, a defence or a document review.

Questions Before Requesting a Consultation
Do I Need to Have an Existing Case?
No. You can request advice before signing a contract, accepting a settlement or entering a partnership, as well as to review an existing claim, case or judgment.
Do You Assist Both Individuals and Businesses?
Yes. We assist individuals, businesses, investors, entrepreneurs and residents with matters connected to the UAE. The engagement may cover a preventive review or work on an existing dispute.
Are Consultations Available in Arabic, English and Russian?
Yes, the service is available in all three languages. Some documents may require certified legal translation for official use, depending on the requirements of the relevant authority.
Can We Work Remotely and Send Documents Electronically?
Initial contact and document exchange can take place remotely. We identify the documents needed for the review, then explain whether any step requires attendance in person, a power of attorney or attestation. An appropriate method for sharing sensitive documents can be agreed.
How Much Does a Consultation Cost?
The fee depends on the type of matter, the volume of documents and the scope of review. We explain our fees before the service begins. Any government charges or third-party service costs are explained when identified and agreed.
Does Sending an Enquiry Mean You Have Accepted My Matter?
Sending a message or document does not, by itself, mean that we have accepted the engagement or begun monitoring legal deadlines. Work begins once the scope of service and fees have been agreed and the engagement accepted.
Does a Consultation Guarantee a Particular Outcome?
No. A consultation helps assess rights, evidence and options, but the outcome depends on the facts, documents, competent authority and procedures. The ability to recover money may also depend on the assets available.
Do Your Services Include Tax, Compliance, Translation and Business Support?
Yes, within the agreed scope of work. We explain what Summit provides directly and what requires a licensed advocate, registered legal translator, tax agent or other professional, depending on the matter.
