Practical Situations We Review with You
Review of Assessments and Penalties
We examine the reason for the assessment or penalty, the tax period, the calculation and the information on which the decision was based. We then review returns, invoices, contracts and records to identify whether the disagreement concerns the facts, legal characterisation, calculation or procedure.
The work includes preparing arguments and legal submissions, organising supporting documents and coordinating with an accountant or registered tax agent where the matter requires it. Legal support alone does not mean that the company is registered as a tax agent.
Choosing the Correct Review Application
Different routes are available, including a tax assessment review, a request for reconsideration, an objection before the Tax Disputes Resolution Committee and then a court challenge where available. Each route has its own requirements, deadlines and relationship with earlier applications.
The Authority’s service states that a request for reconsideration should normally be submitted within 40 business days of notification of the decision, with the possibility of requesting an extension of the filing period under the prescribed rules. An extension must not be assumed. Equally, a blanket statement that deadlines never allow exceptions would be inaccurate. We identify the appropriate route and deadline from your file before submission.
Corporate Tax and Registration
Being subject to tax, having to register and the amount of tax payable are separate questions. A zero tax liability does not automatically remove registration or filing obligations, and free zone companies are not all exempt from the system.
We review the person’s status, activity, period and relevant obligations, and explain which issues require a legal opinion and which require calculations or work by a tax specialist.
How We Handle Your Matter
Review the decision or penalty, its grounds and the related documents.
Check deadlines, the admissibility requirements for each application and any possibility of an extension under the rules, without assuming an exception will be granted.
Gather and organise records: invoices, returns, contracts and account statements.
Develop the legal and financial arguments supporting the position.
Submit the objection or challenge through the appropriate channels.
Documents That Help Us Get Started
The complete decision, assessment or penalty, with proof and date of notification.
Returns, registration details, invoices, contracts, records and payments relating to the period.
Any earlier review or reconsideration request or objection, the response and the supporting documents submitted.
An explanation of the accounting or tax treatment in dispute and any relevant report or regulatory correspondence.
Send clear, complete copies initially and retain the originals. Do not send passwords, verification codes or financial account access credentials. An appropriate method for sharing sensitive documents can be agreed.
Before Taking the Next Step
Useful First Steps
Do not ignore any notice from the Federal Tax Authority; deadlines continue to run
Keep all invoices, contracts and bank records in order
Review your tax position when changing or expanding your activities
Do not respond to the Authority without organising the supporting documents
Mistakes to Avoid
Mixing personal and company accounts
Leaving records disorganised until a tax decision is issued
Ignoring the Authority’s correspondence or delaying a response
Assuming tax is purely an accounting matter that needs no legal review

FAQs
Does Having No Tax to Pay Mean Registration Is Not Required?
Not necessarily. Tax liability, registration, filing and the amount payable are separate questions. The person’s status, activity, period, exemptions and reliefs must be reviewed; profit or the amount payable alone is not enough.
How Do I Request Reconsideration of an Authority Decision?
The decision, notification date and earlier applications must be reviewed first. The Authority’s service states that the request should normally be submitted within 40 business days of notification, with the possibility of requesting an extension of the filing period under the rules. An extension must not be assumed, and an earlier assessment review may affect the sequence of procedures.
Can a Penalty Be Challenged or a Reduction Requested?
A review of the lawfulness of a decision differs from a request for a waiver, instalments or a reduction under the rules of each route. The correct application, documents and deadlines must be identified. One application should not be assumed to preserve the deadline for another automatically.
When Is VAT Registration Required?
Taxable supplies and imports, registration thresholds, periods and exceptions must be reviewed under the rules in force. A general revenue figure or the fact that the business operates in a free zone does not replace that assessment.
What Happens If Reconsideration Is Refused?
An objection before the Tax Disputes Resolution Committee and then a court challenge may be available, subject to the applicable requirements and deadlines. The requirements, earlier procedures and the effect of payment where relevant must be reviewed against the decision and the file.
Is Summit a Registered Tax Agent?
The service described here is legal support in reviewing decisions, arguments and legal submissions, and organising the file. Work requiring a registered tax agent or accounting expertise is coordinated with an appropriately qualified specialist. Court advocacy is undertaken by a licensed advocate where required.
