About Summit Legal Consultancy and Our Team
A careful review of your matter. A clear view of the next step.
Summit Legal Consultancy is a legal consultancy in the United Arab Emirates, supporting individuals, businesses, investors and residents with contracts, claims, disputes and regulatory matters. We combine legal analysis with organised follow-up, explaining what the matter needs and what can be done before agreeing the engagement.
We provide services in Arabic, English and Russian because understanding an issue begins with being able to explain it and discuss its details clearly. The facts, documents and competent authority remain the basis of our advice and proposed next step.

Why Summit Was Founded
A problem may begin with a clause that was not read carefully, an agreement left undocumented or a deadline addressed too late. When a disagreement emerges, the question goes beyond knowing the law: what actually happened, what can be proved, and what is the right decision now?
Summit was founded to help clients understand their legal position clearly, in their own language, while there are still options to consider. We examine each matter in context, grounding our advice in the available documents and the outcome the client seeks.
You may need to protect a business relationship before signing, organise a financial claim, review a judgment or understand the effect of a proposed settlement. The value of advice lies in helping you make an informed decision, with an understanding of its requirements, risks and limits.
What Defines Our Approach
Analysis That Starts with the Facts
We examine the sequence of events, the parties' relationship, contracts, correspondence and payments. We distinguish the account presented in the file from what the evidence supports, so that our advice rests on a picture that can be examined.
A Clear Assessment of Your Legal Position
A claim may be justified in principle but need proof of delivery or entitlement. A position may appear difficult while material documents have not been presented clearly. We explain the strengths, weaknesses and gaps that need to be addressed.
Decisions That Account for Time, Cost and Risk
We weigh negotiation, settlement, claims and formal proceedings according to the matter. We consider how a step may affect deadlines, funds and relationships, and what requires early action or further review.
Preventive Legal Support
Reviewing contracts, defining authority, documenting obligations and checking compliance requirements help reduce uncertainty. Our support for businesses also covers incorporation, partnerships, policies and commercial relationships.
Multilingual Communication
We explain the options in Arabic, English and Russian, taking account of clients' different backgrounds and expectations. Official documents may require certified legal translation, depending on the authority and intended use.
A Defined Engagement and Organised Follow-Up
We explain the scope of work, deliverables, fees and documents needed, and follow up on what the engagement covers. Coordination between management and the legal team helps clients understand what has been done and what decision or document is needed from them.
How We Work on Your Matter
1. We Understand What Happened and What You Need
We start with the parties, chronology and earlier procedures, and identify the question or decision you need help with. Any notice, hearing or response or appeal deadline is given priority in the review.
2. We Review the Material Documents
We examine the relevant contract, cheque, transfer, judgment, correspondence and reports. We identify what is missing and whether versions, dates or details need to be reconciled.
3. We Explain the Position and Options
We explain what supports the claim, potential objections, available options and their requirements. A technical opinion, translation or additional professional expertise may be needed; we explain this where relevant.
4. We Agree the Work and Follow It Through
We define the scope of engagement, deliverables, fees and any agreed external charges or services. We then organise the work and follow-up, explaining developments that require a new decision or a change in scope.

Sarah Zaki examines civil and commercial matters, drafts and reviews contracts and agreements, and prepares submissions and written legal advice. Her work includes analysing documents for individuals and businesses against the relevant federal and local legal frameworks in the UAE.
In reviewing a contract, she gives as much attention to what the text leaves unaddressed as to what it says. Who can approve performance? When does payment fall due? How are changes, termination or disagreements over obligations handled? This approach helps reveal ambiguities that may become the basis of a later dispute.
The matters she works on include company contracts, commercial agreements, memoranda of understanding and personal contracts, as well as corporate documents, internal policies and compliance records. She connects the review of wording to the agreement's purpose and practical performance, so that the document reflects what the parties actually intend to regulate.
When a disagreement exists, she starts by examining the facts, obligations and correspondence to identify the issues that need to be addressed. Her approach combines precision in drafting, document assessment and work towards resolving disputes at an early stage, where the facts and appropriate procedure allow.

Clear Roles in Legal Work
Executive management organises operations, the client experience, coordination and follow-up. Consultants examine the issues and prepare legal advice and documents within the engagement scope. This division connects the client's needs with the work their matter requires.
Summit is a legal consultancy. Advocacy or representation requiring an advocate is undertaken by licensed advocates, according to the authority and procedure. Where a matter requires a technical expert, legal translator or tax agent, that specialist's role and the requirements for their involvement are defined separately and clearly.
A Clear Engagement and Confidential Information
Before work begins, we agree the required service, deliverables and fees, and explain any external charges or work when agreed. Costs and requirements vary with the volume of documents, nature of the matter and stage it has reached.
We handle information and documents in accordance with professional confidentiality and legal obligations, identifying what needs to be disclosed to carry out the engagement. We ask clients to provide all material facts and documents, including those that may not support their position, so that the review rests on an accurate picture.
A consultation is not a promise of a judgment, recovery of funds or a particular outcome. We explain what can be done and the factors on which the outcome depends, and reassess the position when material new information emerges.
Legal Notice and Scope of Services
Understand Your Matter Before Making a Decision
Whether you are reviewing a contract before signing, facing an existing dispute, or need to structure a partnership or claim, send a clear summary and the key documents available. We identify with you the information needed and the appropriate service before agreeing to start work.
If you have a current deadline to respond, appeal or attend, state it clearly when contacting us. Sending an enquiry or documents does not mean that an engagement has been accepted or monitoring of legal deadlines has begun.
