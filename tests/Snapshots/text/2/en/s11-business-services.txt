Practical Situations We Review with You
Choosing a Structure and Licensing Jurisdiction
We review the activity, ownership, location of customers and the need for premises, employees or sector-specific approvals. We then consider the appropriate legal form and mainland or free zone jurisdiction, together with the associated operational and tax requirements.
Full foreign ownership is available for many activities and structures, but approvals and activity-specific restrictions still need to be checked. A free zone licence also does not automatically permit every type of transaction in the local market.
Incorporation and Amendment Procedures
An engagement may include assistance with trade name reservation, preparation of incorporation and authorisation documents, coordination of application submissions and follow-up, adding an activity, updating details or renewing a licence. We explain which work Summit undertakes and which steps require a licensing authority or specialist provider.
Timeframes and costs are determined after identifying the activity, authority, documents and approvals required. We do not promise the issue of a licence or residency permit, or the opening of a bank account, before completing an assessment of the requirements; each authority has its own procedures and decision-making powers.
Organising an Existing Company’s Records
We help organise the memorandum of association, ownership records, authorisations, key contracts and renewals, and prepare documents required for attestation and government transactions. When an investor joins, we identify what needs to be completed before the file is submitted for legal due diligence.
After the Licence Is Issued
We explain the obligations requiring ongoing attention, such as renewals and updates to ownership and beneficial ownership information. Where needed, specialist accounting, tax or regulatory matters are referred for a separate scope of work.
How We Handle Your Matter
Identify the nature of the activity, target market and customer locations.
Select an appropriate incorporation structure: company type and location.
Establish the requirements: capital, documents, timeframe and fees.
Prepare the documents and submit them to the competent authorities.
Follow up on renewals and recurring requirements after incorporation.
Documents That Help Us Get Started
A description of the activity, customers, operating location and proposed or existing ownership structure.
Identity and ownership documents, the licence, memorandum of association and records, where a company already exists.
Authorisations, resolutions, previous approvals, premises agreements and documents relevant to the competent authority.
A list of current obligations, contracts, assets and renewal or amendment deadlines, where relevant to the engagement.
Send clear, complete copies initially and retain the originals. Do not send passwords, verification codes or financial account access credentials. An appropriate method for sharing sensitive documents can be agreed.
Before Taking the Next Step
Useful First Steps
Define your activity precisely before licensing; ambiguity can be costly.
Keep organised copies of all company documents.
Check the licence before undertaking any new activity.
Do not postpone licence renewal; penalties accumulate.
Mistakes to Avoid
Choosing an incorporation structure solely on price without considering the activity
Undertaking an activity not listed on the licence, even if it appears related
Failing to organise company documents before an investor or partner joins
Delaying renewals until penalties exceed the fees

FAQs
Should I Choose a Free Zone or the Mainland?
Compare the options based on your activity, market, customers, premises, employees, approvals and budget. Check the operational and tax rules, and do not assume either option is always better or tax-exempt.
How Long Does Company Formation Take and What Does It Cost?
This is determined after identifying the activity, authority, legal form, documents and approvals. We explain service fees, government charges and known external costs, without guaranteeing a licence, bank account or residency permit by an unconfirmed date.
Can the Activity Be Changed After Licensing?
An amendment may be requested under the licensing authority’s procedures and subject to the necessary approvals. Check the requirements before starting the new activity and review its effect on the company’s other obligations and contracts.
What Should I Put in Order Before an Investor Joins?
The licence, memorandum of association, ownership register, authorisations, contracts, obligations, financial and tax records, and rights to products or brands. Disclose any unregistered promises of shares or rights so that due diligence reflects the actual position.
Does Company Formation Cover All Other Obligations?
The issue of a licence marks the start of operations, not the end of ongoing obligations. Depending on the activity and applicable regime, tax registration or filing, information and beneficial ownership updates, renewals and sector-specific approvals may be required.
How Does This Service Differ from Corporate and Contract Services?
This service focuses on structure, licensing procedures, documents and operational coordination. Corporate and contract services address partners’ rights, agreements and disputes; an engagement may combine both areas.
