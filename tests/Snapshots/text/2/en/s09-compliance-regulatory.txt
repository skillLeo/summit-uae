Practical Situations We Review with You
Identifying Requirements from the Actual Business Activity
We start with the legal form, licensing location, activities, customers, products and flow of funds. From those facts, we identify the relevant authorities and rules rather than using the same checklist for every company.
Being established in a free zone does not necessarily exclude federal legislation. Financial free zones also have different regimes from other free zones, and some obligations depend on the activity or transaction rather than the licence description alone.
Anti-Money Laundering and Due Diligence
For businesses within scope, we review risk assessments, customer acceptance policies, beneficial owner identification, verification of information, record retention, training and related procedures. Reporting duties and responsibilities are determined by the applicable rules and supervisory authority.
The assessment includes whether actual files are consistent with policy: what was collected, who reviewed it and how risk indicators were addressed. A signed form alone does not establish that the requirements have been fully implemented.
Governance, Data and Licensing
We help review signing authority, management delegations, the handling of conflicts of interest, beneficial ownership requirements and policies for handling customer and employee data. We prioritise corrective action according to risk and responsibility for implementation.
If You Receive a Regulatory Enquiry
Keep the complete request and establish the response deadline. Do not create backdated records or alter information to suggest compliance that did not occur. We help prepare an accurate response explaining the facts, documents and a remediation plan where deficiencies exist.
Engagement deliverables may include a list of applicable obligations, a gap analysis, updates to specified policies and a remediation plan identifying priorities, responsible persons, deadlines and evidence of completion. Completing the review does not amount to regulatory approval or guarantee that no penalty will be imposed.
How We Handle Your Matter
Review the licence and permitted activities and compare them with actual operations.

Identify gaps between regulatory requirements and actual practice.
Develop a remediation plan with clear priorities and a timetable.
Review documentation, training and internal procedures.
Documents That Help Us Get Started
The licence, incorporation documents, ownership and control structure, and authorisations.
A description of activities, products, customers, countries, the flow of funds and sector-specific approvals.
Risk, due diligence, beneficial ownership and data retention policies, together with training and implementation records.
Supervisory requests, earlier reports, remediation plans and evidence that actions have been completed.
Send clear, complete copies initially and retain the originals. Do not send passwords, verification codes or financial account access credentials. An appropriate method for sharing sensitive documents can be agreed.

FAQs
Are All Companies Subject to the Same AML Requirements?
No. Obligations differ according to the activity, the capacity in which the business acts and its supervisory authority. The rules cover financial sectors and specified non-financial businesses and professions within their scope. The actual activities and services must be checked rather than copying another company’s policy.
Does Being in a Free Zone Exclude Federal Laws?
Not necessarily. Financial free zones have different regimes from other free zones, and federal obligations may apply according to the activity or transaction. Each applicable framework and authority must be identified from the business’s circumstances.
Can We Carry On an Activity Similar to the Licensed Activity?
Do not assume that commercial similarity is enough. Review the activity description, approvals and restrictions. A licence amendment or additional permit may be needed before starting the activity.
When Should Beneficial Ownership Information Be Reviewed?
When ownership, control or material information changes, and as part of the regular reviews required by the applicable rules. The procedure, deadline and authority must be identified for the particular circumstances.
Is a Written Policy Enough?
Businesses subject to an obligation must implement the requirements and retain evidence. The review covers customer files, risk assessment, verification, training, escalation and records, rather than the existence of a form alone.
How Should I Handle a Regulatory Enquiry?
Keep the complete request and identify its scope, deadline and the person responsible for responding. Provide accurate information, valid documents and a remediation plan for any deficiencies, without concealing information or creating records suggesting earlier compliance that did not occur.
