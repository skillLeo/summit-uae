Practical Situations We Review with You
Identifying the Rules Governing the Employment Relationship
The following rules concern full-time foreign employees in the private sector who are subject to the Federal Labour Law. Other rules and systems exist, including those of the Dubai International Financial Centre and Abu Dhabi Global Market, government employment, domestic workers and alternative savings schemes where applicable.
Salaries and Final Settlement
We review unpaid wages, notice pay, accrued leave, end-of-service gratuity, commissions and contractual payments according to their terms. We then reconcile the calculation with transfer records and actual payments and deductions.
Under the Federal Labour Law, wages and other amounts due on termination must be paid within 14 days of the contract ending. Establishing that date requires a review of the termination letter, the notice period and how it was implemented, rather than relying solely on the residence cancellation date.
End-of-Service Gratuity and Resignation
Under the traditional scheme for full-time foreign employees, entitlement begins after one year of continuous service. Gratuity is calculated on the basic wage at 21 days for each of the first five years and 30 days for each subsequent year, subject to pro rata calculation for part-years, the statutory cap and periods excluded from the calculation.
Resignation alone does not reduce this gratuity under the current federal scheme. However, the way employment ends may affect notice pay, compensation or other claims. We therefore assess those items separately rather than combining them with gratuity.
Releases and Ending Employment
Do not acknowledge receipt of money that has not been paid. We review whether the wording of a release records receipt, contains a settlement or waiver, and complies with statutory rights. Signing does not mean that all rights are lost in every case, but it may create an evidential dispute that accurate wording and proof of payment can avoid.
Employment Complaints and Defence
We identify the claims, the competent authority and the route for a complaint or challenge to a decision according to the amount and nature of the claim. For employers, we review performance, termination and payment records, and compliance with the contract before preparing a response.
Send the employment contract, termination or resignation letter, payslips and transfer records, leave balance, and any previous settlement or complaint.
The review also covers probation, termination terms and moving to another job under the applicable rules. Do not assume that the notice period is the same in every case or that residence cancellation alone determines when the employment contract ended. Documents and dates must be considered together before entitlements are calculated.
How We Handle Your Matter
Review the employment contract: basic salary, allowances, contract term and termination conditions.
Review payslips and bank transfers to establish what has and has not been paid.
Gather relevant correspondence with human resources or the employer.
Establish all entitlements: end-of-service gratuity, leave days and allowances.
Choose the appropriate route: negotiation, settlement or a complaint to the competent authority.
Documents That Help Us Get Started
The employment contract and its schedules, and any amendments to pay, commission or benefits.
Payslips, transfer records, a record of payments and deductions, and the leave balance.

Any earlier complaint, decision or judgment, proof of service, and any current deadline for a response or challenge.
Send clear, complete copies initially and retain the originals. Do not send passwords, verification codes or financial account access credentials. An appropriate method for sharing sensitive documents can be agreed.

FAQs
How Is End-of-Service Gratuity Calculated?
For a full-time foreign employee under the traditional federal scheme, entitlement begins after one year of continuous service. It is calculated on the basic wage at 21 days for each of the first five years and 30 days for each subsequent year, subject to part-years, statutory limits and excluded periods. Other systems and alternative savings schemes differ.
Does Resignation Reduce Gratuity?
Resignation alone does not reduce gratuity under the current federal scheme referred to above. Other amounts, such as notice pay or compensation, may differ, so each element should be reviewed separately from gratuity.
What Should I Do If My Salary Is Late?
Identify the months affected, the wage due and any partial payments, and gather the contract, account statements and correspondence. Then review the appropriate complaint channel and the rules governing the employment relationship, without acknowledging receipt of unpaid amounts.
Do I Need an Advocate to File an Employment Complaint?
You can use the competent channels directly where this is permitted. Advice can help with calculations, drafting claims and reviewing a decision or deadline for challenge. Any subsequent representation depends on the authority and procedure involved.
Should I Sign a Release Before Receiving Payment?
Do not acknowledge receipt of money you have not received. Ask for the figures and wording to match actual payment. Not every signature extinguishes all rights, but it may create an avoidable evidential dispute.
Do the Same Rules Apply During Probation?
Probation has its own rules on termination, notice and changing jobs, and the position may vary with the circumstances and the applicable system. Review the contract, dates and next employment destination before submitting a resignation or deciding to terminate employment.
