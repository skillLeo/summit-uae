Frequently Asked Legal Questions in the UAE
Clear answers to help you identify your next step
Is a cheque enough to begin enforcement? What should you check before signing an employment settlement? Where should you start if you suspect digital fraud? We have brought together questions frequently asked by individuals, businesses, investors and residents, with answers explaining the key issues and the documents that help assess them.
Choose the topic closest to your situation, or search using a word from your question. These are general answers in the context of the legal systems applicable in the UAE. The outcome may vary with the emirate, competent authority, type of contract, deadlines and facts. Advice on your situation requires a review of your file, rather than a similarity between its subject and one of these questions.

Before Requesting a Consultation
1. When do I need legal advice?
When a contract, dispute or decision affects your money, business or legal position, or when there is a deadline to respond or challenge a decision. A review is also useful before accepting a settlement or signing an obligation that may be difficult to change later.
The clearer the facts and deadlines, the sooner the scope of the review can be identified. If you have received a court notice or official decision, state its date at the outset. Requesting a consultation does not, by itself, suspend any deadline.
2. Do I need to have an ongoing case?
You can seek advice to review a contract, structure a partnership or prepare a will before any dispute arises, as well as to review an existing claim or judgment. Identify the decision you need to make so that the review has a clear purpose.
The required outcome may be an explanation of a clause, a list of transaction risks, a document review or identification of a claim procedure. The scope of work, deliverables and fees are agreed before the engagement begins, according to the nature of the matter.
3. What documents should I prepare before a consultation?
Start with the document that gave rise to the issue, such as the contract, cheque or decision, then add the related correspondence and payments. Send any court notice or notification establishing a deadline, together with a brief chronology of events.
Arrange the files by date and explain whether any documents are missing or another version differs from yours. At first contact, send what is needed to understand the issue. Do not send passwords, verification codes or cryptocurrency wallet keys.
4. Is a short message enough to assess my position?
It is enough to identify the type of issue and the documents needed. An opinion on the strength of a claim or the appropriate procedure requires a review of the material relevant to that decision. A matter's prospects cannot be assessed from its title alone.
Identify the parties, the subject of the dispute, the amount where relevant, and any steps already taken. If your question concerns a judgment, attach the full judgment and its reasoning, with evidence of when it was served on you, rather than only an image of the operative part.
5. Are services available in Arabic, English and Russian?
Yes, services are available in all three languages. Official procedures may require certified legal translations of certain documents. This is a separate step determined by the authority and the language required.
State your preferred language when requesting an appointment. Having a consultation in a particular language does not mean that the authority will accept all documents in that language or without translation.
6. Does a consultation guarantee the outcome?
A consultation assesses rights, evidence and options. It does not guarantee a court or authority's decision, or the other party's ability to pay. Specific work and clear deliverables can be agreed, with an explanation of the factors on which the outcome depends.
Good work explains the strengths, weaknesses and matters requiring proof, and distinguishes a right that can be claimed from an amount that can actually be recovered. Any preliminary assessment may change if material facts or documents that were previously unavailable come to light.

Debts and Cheques
7. How do I start recovering a commercial debt?
Identify the debtor, outstanding balance and due date, then collect the contract, invoices, evidence of performance, payments and correspondence. The suitability of settlement, a formal notice, court proceedings or enforcement can then be reviewed against the documents and jurisdiction.
Also check the contracting party's legal name and its representative's authority, and separate the principal debt from any additional amounts claimed. A clear statement of account showing each payment and objection helps prevent the dispute over the calculation itself from escalating.
8. Is every bounced cheque a criminal matter?
Insufficient funds alone do not automatically amount to a criminal offence. Enforcement on the cheque may be available, while other criminalised acts may require separate criminal proceedings. The reason for dishonour, circumstances of use and relevant legal provisions must be reviewed.
Article 667 of the Commercial Transactions Law provides for treating a cheque bearing the bank's statement that funds are absent or insufficient as an executory instrument, subject to that article's provisions. Reviewing enforcement on a cheque therefore differs from examining an allegation of forgery or other potentially criminal conduct.
9. Why should I keep the original cheque and the bank's statement?
To establish the cheque details and reason for non-payment, and provide what the procedure requires. The bank's statement may affect the available enforcement route. Also keep evidence of payments and the underlying relationship to assess any dispute about the obligation.
If the cheque was replaced or a partial payment made, document the connection between the records and the remaining balance. Do not discard or hand over an earlier document without recording what was agreed and what is to happen to any ongoing proceedings.
10. Can I pursue a claim if the debtor has left the UAE?
A claim or enforcement proceedings may still be possible, particularly where assets remain in the UAE. The options depend on jurisdiction, asset location and the enforceability of judgments. The debtor's departure is no reason to neglect preserving documents or observing deadlines.
The practicality and cost of a cross-border route, and the need for proceedings in another country, must also be assessed. Having a judgment or instrument does not mean it can automatically be enforced in every country or against every asset.
11. Is an invoice alone enough?
Its evidential value depends on how it was issued, whether it was accepted and its connection to established dealings. A purchase order, delivery evidence, service acceptance or acknowledgement of the balance strengthens the claim. An invoice issued by one party does not resolve every objection to entitlement.
If the customer disputed quantity or quality, keep the objection, your response and evidence of how the issues were addressed. Withholding that correspondence from the legal review may lead to an incomplete assessment of the claim's strength.
12. How should I document a repayment plan?
Specify the debt, outstanding balance, instalments, due dates, payment method, security, consequences of default, and what is to happen to existing cheques or proceedings. Check the company representative's signing authority and the agreement's effect on your existing rights.
Also clarify whether the agreement merely reschedules payment or includes a waiver or final settlement, and when each obligation is treated as performed. Do not assume that accepting an instalment closes a case or suspends existing enforcement without the required procedural step.
13. When should I choose formal action over negotiation?
When negotiations do not produce a serious agreement, a legal deadline is approaching or recovery is at risk. The decision balances the strength of the evidence, cost, assets and security. Negotiations may continue in parallel where legally appropriate.
Repeated promises to pay do not necessarily preserve legal deadlines. Give negotiations an objective, timeframe and written proposal, and assess how waiting affects the evidence, security and access to funds.

Criminal Cases
14. What should I do if summoned by the police or prosecution?
Verify the authority and appointment through official channels and follow the instructions. Prepare the documents and chronology, and seek an assessment of your role and the legal help you may need, without delaying attendance or destroying any evidence.
Do not disclose sensitive information or make a payment in response to an unverified call. If language is a barrier, explain that you need appropriate language assistance, and do not sign a statement whose contents you do not understand.
15. Does my name appearing in a document prove my involvement?
It does not, on its own, establish the elements of an allegation. The document's authenticity and context, your role and knowledge, and the other evidence must be examined. It nevertheless requires a precise explanation, rather than being ignored or denied without review.
The significance of a signature, attendance or receipt of money may vary according to what you were asked to do and what you knew. Presenting each person's role separately helps avoid confusing shared events with individual responsibility.
16. Can an earlier criminal judgment be reviewed?
The available appeal or review procedure must be assessed against the judgment's type, level, deadlines and reasoning. Send the judgment, service notices and any previous challenge. Re-examining a file does not mean that a retrial is available in every case.
It also matters whether the judgment was given in the defendant's presence or absence, whether it has already been challenged, and the date of the last procedural step. New evidence alone does not reopen every judgment or override its deadlines.
17. What is the difference between criminal and civil proceedings?
Criminal proceedings address responsibility for conduct criminalised by law. Civil or commercial proceedings address rights, obligations and claims to enforce them. The proceedings may concern the same facts, but each has its own conditions, procedures and effects of judgments.
An outcome in one set of proceedings must not be read beyond its scope. A financial claim may be decided while another procedure remains ongoing. The judgment's effect, finality and connection to the matter under consideration must be examined.
18. Can WhatsApp conversations be used as evidence?
They may form part of the evidence, subject to examination of authenticity, attribution, completeness, context and the manner of submission. Keep the original conversation and related files. Do not rely on an extract that changes the meaning or omits material replies.
Keep the device or available original copy, account details, dates and attachments. Submission in the required form or technical examination may be needed if the evidence is disputed. A screenshot alone is not sufficient in every matter.
19. How do I demonstrate good faith when accused of fraud or forgery?
Explain your role and what you knew when acting, linking this to the correspondence, transactions and chronology. The defence is assessed against the elements of the offence, while the burden of proving the allegation remains with the competent authority.
It helps to explain who asked you to act, what you were told and how the money or documents moved. What is needed is an accurate account supported by evidence, rather than a general assertion of good faith that leaves important facts unexplained.
20. Should I contact the complainant to discuss settlement?
Whether this is appropriate depends on the allegation, procedural stage and any restrictions in force. Do not threaten, apply pressure or ask for statements to be changed. A financial agreement also does not automatically bring every criminal case to an end.
When considering settlement, document its scope and effect on the financial claim and related proceedings. Do not promise a procedural outcome that the other party does not have sole authority to determine.

Cryptocurrencies
21. What should I do if I suspect fraud through a platform?
Stop making new payments, promptly notify the bank or platform and the appropriate official channels, and preserve evidence at the same time. Prepare the transactions, conversations and recipient's identity. Do not share your wallet recovery phrase or verification codes.
Do not wait until every document is ready if a transaction may still be stopped or your account remains at risk. Use the contact details on the authority or organisation's official website, and be wary of anyone asking for another payment in return for guaranteed recovery.
22. Can stolen cryptocurrency be recovered?
The options depend on the ability to trace the transaction, identify the responsible party, establish jurisdiction and locate assets, as well as the cooperation available from platforms. Traceability is not the same as recoverability. An outcome cannot properly be guaranteed before the matter is understood.
Depending on the facts, the first step may be notifying a platform, filing a complaint or seeking an appropriate measure to preserve rights. Assess the cost and purpose of tracing before engaging any provider, and verify its identity and scope of service.
23. I sold USDT and then faced a complaint. Am I liable?
Selling and delivering cryptocurrency do not, on their own, prove fraud or exclude every possible liability. The review considers the identities of the counterparty and payer, promises made, source of funds, your knowledge and role, and relevant regulatory obligations.
Distinguish the sale from any investment promise made by someone else, while explaining the actual connection between them. The transaction may involve separate civil and criminal proceedings. An outcome in one must not automatically be carried over to the other.
24. What is the key evidence in a cryptocurrency dispute?
The trade order and platform record, TxID, network and wallet addresses, bank transfers and complete conversations. Each payment must be linked to the transaction and recipient, distinguishing what the blockchain establishes from what requires off-chain identity information.
Keep the transaction time and time zone, order numbers and available recipient details. Do not send a private key or recovery phrase to prove wallet ownership. Appropriate verification methods that do not disclose control credentials can be considered.
25. When do I need a technical expert?
When a material technical issue arises, such as the transfer trail, control of an account or reconciliation of platform data. The expert's task and questions must be defined. A technical opinion alone does not replace an assessment of legal liability.
Data sources must be open to examination, and the report must distinguish confirmed information from inference and explain the limits of tracing. Not every digital dispute needs an independent expert report if the issue in question is of a different nature.
26. Is dealing in cryptocurrency permitted in the UAE?
The position depends on the activity, location and capacity in which a person acts. Trading for one's own account differs from providing services to others, promotion or management. Identify the regulator and required licence rather than assuming that every transaction is permitted or prohibited.
In Dubai, the Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority's remit covers the emirate and its free zones, excluding the Dubai International Financial Centre. That jurisdiction does not automatically extend to all emirates or remove the need to examine the activity and relevant approvals.
27. When does a digital dispute become a criminal matter?
When facts and evidence support a suspicion of criminal conduct, such as fraud, forgery or money laundering. A loss or difficulty withdrawing funds alone does not determine the legal classification. The promises, conduct, relationship and source of funds must be reviewed.
The disagreement may concern a contract, withdrawal condition or commercial breach, and a separate suspicion of criminal conduct may also exist. The correct classification requires an understanding of what the customer was promised and what actually happened, rather than simply the platform's name or cryptocurrency type.
28. How do I address a frozen account linked to trading?
Identify who imposed the restriction, why, and what the bank or authority has requested. Prepare evidence of the source of funds, contracts, trade orders and transfer records. A bank compliance review requires a different response from an order issued by an investigating authority or court.
A bank's request for transaction information does not, on its own, establish a crime. Responding to the bank also does not automatically lift a court order. Identify the decision-maker first, then provide a documented and consistent explanation of the movement of funds.

Companies and Contracts
29. What should I arrange before setting up a company with a partner?
Equity interests, contributions, authority, profits, rights in the product or project, withdrawal arrangements and dispute resolution. Align the agreement with the incorporation and registration documents, and specify when and how any rights or assets transfer to the company.
Also agree on additional funding, conflicts of interest, decision-making deadlock and a partner's exit. Clearly distinguish a registered equity interest from a promise of a future interest or an interest to be granted for services where that arrangement has not yet been implemented.
30. Is an oral agreement binding?
It may create an obligation in some circumstances, but certain transactions require a specific form or registration, and proof may be disputed. Documenting the terms is preferable, especially where ownership, equity interests, security or valuable rights are involved.
If dealings took place without a written contract, collect the correspondence and evidence of performance and payment. Do not assume that being able to prove an oral agreement substitutes for the form or registration that the law requires for the transaction itself.
31. Which clauses matter most in a contract review?
The subject of the obligation, delivery, acceptance, payment, liability, warranties, termination, intellectual property and dispute resolution. Annexes, definitions and inconsistencies must also be examined, with the clauses assessed against how the transaction will actually be performed.
Check how acceptable delivery is defined, who may approve the work, and how changes and delays are handled. A general statement about providing a service may leave a substantial disagreement when an invoice is issued or termination requested.
32. How do I begin addressing a dispute with a partner?
Collect the constitutional documents, partners' agreement, management decisions, correspondence and relevant accounts. Identify the disputed action and the remedy sought. Do not change authority or transfer assets without reviewing your legal basis.
Distinguish financial disagreements from disputes over management or ownership, and identify any need for urgent action or risk to company documents. The options may vary with the constitutional documents, partners' agreement and the authority with which the company is registered.
33. What is the difference between a free zone and the mainland?
Licensing, operational scope, legal form and approvals vary with the authority and activity. Federal legislation may apply alongside free-zone rules. No single answer suits every business, and there is no blanket tax exemption for all free zones.
Start with what the company will do, where and with whom, then compare the alternatives. Rules on dealings outside the zone, premises, employees, sector supervision and taxes may matter more in practice than a difference in incorporation fees.
34. Can a contract be reviewed after signing?
Yes, to interpret obligations and identify performance requirements, agreed amendments, or options for termination and claims. Signing does not permit unilateral changes to the terms, nor does it prevent examination of a clause's invalidity or unenforceability where a legal basis exists.
A review may also help document an agreed amendment or interpret a clause before it becomes a dispute. Keep the signed version, all annexes and any correspondence about changes or objections to performance.

Property and Tenancy
35. What is the first step when property handover is delayed?
Identify the contractual date and extension clauses, collect payment records and correspondence, and check official project information. Then assess the breach and appropriate remedy, rather than relying only on a date in an advertisement or sales message.
Distinguish the completion percentage shown in an official enquiry from the binding contractual handover date. Provide any earlier notice, complaint or settlement offer so that deadlines and options can be considered in their full context.
36. Am I entitled to terminate because of delay?
Termination may be sought where legal and contractual grounds exist, taking into account the extent of the breach, both parties' obligations and required notices. Delay does not create an automatic entitlement in every matter. Stopping payments requires a separate assessment.
Depending on the facts and available grounds, the appropriate remedy may be performance, compensation or termination. Do not sign an amendment to the handover date or a settlement without understanding whether it waives earlier claims.
37. What should I do if I discover a defect in a property or car?
Document the defect, when it appeared and earlier disclosures, and obtain an appropriate report where needed. Review the defect's nature, whether it predated the sale, the buyer's knowledge, warranties and deadlines. The remedy may be repair, compensation or rescission, depending on the circumstances.
If the defect affects safety, address that first while preserving the evidence that can be retained. Do not rush into repairs that erase the cause of the dispute, or sell the item before documenting its condition and obtaining an appropriate assessment.
38. Is immediate eviction permitted?
There must be a ground and procedure under the applicable legal system. In Dubai, grounds for eviction during the tenancy differ from certain grounds at its expiry, as do notice requirements. Do not apply one notice period to every situation.
Keep the notice, the method of sending, receipt date and stated reason, then assess its effect on your contract. A party's demand to vacate does not, by itself, make forcible eviction or changing the locks lawful.
39. Why review a lease or sale contract before signing?
To establish delivery, maintenance, warranties, payments, termination, penalties and jurisdiction, and ensure material promises appear in the text. Documenting the condition of the property, facilities and included items is useful, rather than relying on an oral description.
Also check the signing party's capacity and authority to let or sell, and identify refundable amounts and conditions for deductions. Important promises should be clearly included in the contractual documents.
40. Where are tenancy disputes filed in Dubai?
The Rental Disputes Center has jurisdiction over many tenancy disputes in Dubai, subject to exceptions and the limits of its remit. The contract, property and remedy sought must be examined, as the route may differ from a sale or development dispute.
Identifying whether the claim concerns rent, eviction, a deposit or repairs, and providing the contract, registration and correspondence, helps select the correct procedure. Purchasing a unit from a developer requires a separate assessment of the dispute and competent forum.

Employment and Entitlements
41. What should I do if my employer has not paid my salary?
Identify the months in arrears, wage amount and partial payments, and collect the contract, account statements and correspondence. Check the competent complaint channel and applicable legal system. Do not acknowledge receipt of unpaid amounts or decide to resign without assessing the consequences.
If the claim includes commissions, end-of-service gratuity or pay in lieu of notice, calculate and document each component separately. State whether there has been an earlier complaint, settlement or decision, as this may affect the next step and its deadlines.
42. How is end-of-service gratuity calculated?
For a full-time foreign worker covered by the traditional federal regime, entitlement arises after one year of continuous service and is calculated on basic salary at 21 days for each of the first five years and 30 days for each subsequent year, subject to the remaining legal conditions and limits.
After one full year of service, fractions of a year are calculated proportionately. Days of unpaid absence are excluded under the applicable rules, and the statutory cap applies. Different treatment may apply to part-time work, alternative savings schemes or special employment regimes. The applicable system must therefore be identified before calculating.
43. Does resignation reduce end-of-service gratuity?
Resignation alone does not reduce gratuity under the current federal regime referred to above. Keep gratuity separate from notice pay, deductions and other compensation, and check the applicable regime and any alternative savings programme.
Do not rely on old tables linking the gratuity percentage to years of service on resignation without checking the law in force. A separate dispute may remain over length of service, basic salary or amounts owed by either party.
44. Should I refuse to sign an inaccurate final settlement acknowledgement?
Do not acknowledge receiving what you have not received. Ask for the figures and wording to be corrected and payment documented. The legality of waiving statutory rights may require examination, but avoiding an inaccurate acknowledgement is easier than disputing its effect later.
Request a breakdown of wages, gratuity, notice pay, leave and amounts paid. If you have already signed, keep the document and payment evidence and have them reviewed. Do not assume either that the signature settles everything or that it has no effect.
45. Is termination without notice permitted?
It is permitted in cases specified by law and subject to their conditions. Failure to meet those conditions may give rise to notice pay or other entitlements. The reasons, documents and procedure must be reviewed. Not every sudden termination can be assumed lawful or unlawful.
Distinguish termination during probation, termination with notice, and dismissal without notice for specified reasons. The applicable legal regime and documents determine whether the procedures and conditions have been met.
46. How does resignation differ from termination by the employer?
Notice pay, the basis for claiming compensation and mutual obligations may differ. Federal gratuity, however, is not reduced merely because the employee resigns. We examine each component separately and identify the contract end date, notice period and work actually performed during it.
Keep the resignation or termination letter, proof of delivery, the response and any agreement shortening the notice period. Do not confuse the last day of attendance with the end of the employment relationship where the documents or agreement specify different dates.
47. Do I need a lawyer to submit an employment complaint?
You may use the competent channels directly where they allow this. Advice may help with calculations, drafting claims, or reviewing an earlier decision or appeal deadline. Any later representation depends on the authority and procedure.
The channel varies according to the authority governing the employment. Do not apply Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation procedures to every special regime. Prepare your details, contract, claim calculation and any earlier correspondence or decisions.
48. Can my employer's messages support a claim?
They may clarify a wage, commission, acknowledgement of late payment or reason for termination, and are read alongside the contract and transfers. Keep them in full and in their original source, including replies, attachments and dates.
Link the message to the amount or period it concerns, and check whether a reply includes an objection or condition. Keeping the full discussion helps explain a possible acknowledgement and its limits, rather than relying on a single sentence.

Tax Matters
49. When do I need a legal tax review?
When an assessment or penalty is issued, a disagreement arises over the interpretation of a transaction or procedure, or before submitting a review request or objection. Preparing accounts and returns may also require an accountant or qualified tax agent alongside the legal opinion.
Prepare the decision and notification, returns, contracts, invoices, correspondence and material records. Identifying the legal point in dispute at the outset helps direct the accounting work and collection of documents needed for the response.
50. How do I request a review of a tax decision?
Start by identifying the decision and the date you were notified. Under the Federal Tax Authority's reconsideration service, the general rule is to submit the request within 40 business days of notification of the original decision, stating the reasons and providing supporting documents. An extension of the submission period may be requested under the applicable rules, but acceptance is not automatic.
If a tax assessment review request has already been submitted, check the procedural sequence. A reconsideration request must not be filed before the decision on that review is issued or its statutory determination period, including any extension, has expired. Keep proof of notification and submission. Do not wait for an extension decision on the assumption that the request preserved the deadline.
51. What happens after a reconsideration request is rejected?
An objection before the Tax Disputes Resolution Committee, followed by a challenge before the competent court, may be available subject to the relevant conditions and deadlines. Admission at each stage is not automatic. Review the decision, notification, objection requirements and any applicable payment conditions.
Simply sending the earlier request to another body is not enough without reviewing the reason for rejection and the requirements for the next stage. Distinguish procedural deadlines from the time needed to prepare documents or seek advice.
52. What is the difference between corporate tax and VAT?
Corporate tax concerns taxable income under its rules, while VAT concerns supplies and imports under its own regime. Registration, calculation and filing requirements differ. A low amount payable does not mean there is no duty to register or submit information.
A business may have obligations under both taxes. Related-party transactions, cross-border dealings and free-zone transactions also require their conditions to be examined, rather than relying only on the activity description or place of licensing.
53. Can I challenge a penalty or request relief?
Different routes may exist for reviewing a decision, requesting a waiver or seeking instalments under the applicable rules. Identify the correct application, grounds, documents and deadlines. A request for relief does not automatically replace a challenge to the decision's legality.
Distinguish an argument that the penalty is not legally due from a request to address its effects through an available waiver or instalment procedure. Each has different grounds and supporting documents, and a separate objection deadline may still need to be preserved.
54. What weakens a tax matter?
Missing transaction documents, discrepancies between returns and records, ignored notices, and applications unsuited to the decision or filed late. Organising the legal and accounting records together helps identify and address the disagreement.
Keep a copy of what was submitted, the submission date, application number and later responses, and reconcile the figures across invoices, contracts and accounts. A complete file is not defined by the number of attachments; relevance and clarity matter most.
55. What support does Summit provide in tax disputes?
Reviewing decisions and their legal basis, preparing submissions and supporting documents, and assisting with review requests and objections. Advocacy is undertaken by a licensed advocate where required. Work requiring a registered tax agent or accounting expertise is coordinated separately.
The engagement scope is defined after reviewing the tax type, decision, stage and deadlines. The service does not guarantee cancellation of an assessment or penalty, or release from filing and payment obligations that remain due under the applicable regime.

Family and Wills
56. When is a will useful for a non-Muslim resident?
When you wish to arrange a particular distribution of assets, appoint an executor or make arrangements for minors where the law allows. Eligibility, the registration authority, applicable law, assets and foreign wills must be reviewed. One generic document does not suit every situation.
Prepare a list of assets, their locations, ownership shares and encumbrances, and identify beneficiaries, substitutes and the executor. A will does not transfer ownership during your lifetime or override third-party rights established by law.
57. Does Islamic inheritance law automatically apply to non-Muslims?
That cannot be assumed in every case. Civil frameworks and choice-of-law rules exist, and the position may be affected by nationality, religion, residence, assets, wills and any permitted choice of law. The matter must be assessed without a predetermined assumption.
The outcome may differ for assets in the UAE and abroad, or where earlier documents and joint arrangements exist. The review therefore starts with family and asset details and the potentially applicable law before drafting is proposed.
58. Where can a non-Muslim's will be registered?
Routes include the DIFC Courts Wills Service and competent local authorities, including the Abu Dhabi Judicial Department and Dubai Courts, depending on eligibility and procedure. The route is selected after checking current requirements and the document's scope.
Language, attendance, signing and registration procedures, and asset coverage may differ. Preparing a draft or opening an online account does not mean the will is registered. The procedure must be completed and proof of registration obtained.
59. What happens if someone dies without a will?
Procedures establish the heirs, assets, liabilities and applicable law before distribution under the relevant rules. The ability to deal with certain assets may be affected until those procedures are completed. The absence of a will does not mean there is no legal system of inheritance.
The effect of death depends on the type of asset, ownership, related rights and the relevant authority's procedures. Advance planning helps organise information and record intentions, but does not exempt beneficiaries from the procedures required after death.
60. Can a registered will be amended?
It can be amended or replaced under the registration authority's procedures and conditions. Review the effect on earlier documents. Do not merely amend a personal copy while the registered version no longer reflects your current wishes.
Also review it when your family or assets change, such as on marriage, divorce, the birth of a child or acquisition of a significant asset. If you have wills in several countries, examine any revocation clause so that another document is not inadvertently revoked.
61. How do I prepare a custody or maintenance matter?
Collect family documents, any judgment or agreement, and evidence of income, housing, education, treatment and expenditure. Set out practical requests concerning the children, and review any travel restriction or existing arrangement before taking unilateral action.
Distinguish actual expenses from amounts sought for the future, and support each component with evidence. Children's matters are assessed under the applicable legal regime, their interests and the facts, rather than general promises of a fixed outcome for either parent.

Compliance
62. When should I review my company's compliance?
On incorporation, changes in activity, ownership, customers or products, and receipt of a regulatory enquiry. A business also needs periodic monitoring proportionate to its risks and the competent authority's requirements, rather than waiting for an obvious breach.
Start by identifying the regulator and requirements that actually apply to the activity. Compare them with daily operations, and establish responsibilities, gaps, remediation deadlines and records demonstrating implementation.
63. Who is the ultimate beneficial owner?
The natural person who ultimately owns or controls the entity under the applicable criteria. The review may go beyond the name on the licence to examine ownership structure and actual control, taking account of exceptions and the relevant register's requirements.
Keep records explaining indirect ownership and any arrangements conferring actual control, and review the duty to update information when it changes. Merely copying the names of the apparent partners is insufficient if they do not reflect the actual position under the applicable rules.
64. Is a written anti-money laundering policy enough?
Businesses subject to the requirements must implement them and retain evidence, including due diligence, risk assessment, training and case-related procedures. The policy must suit the activity, and those responsible must understand how to apply it.
Requirements may include updating customer information, monitoring dealings and reporting under the applicable rules. Copying a policy from a different activity may leave a gap between the document and the company's actual risks.
65. Can I carry out an unlisted activity because it is similar to mine?
Do not assume it is permitted. Check the activity description, licence conditions and additional approvals. A licence amendment, permit or sector approval may be required before starting, even if the activity is commercially connected to your current work.
This also applies to a new service offered online, through an intermediary or via a platform. Check with the relevant authority before advertising or contracting. A customer's acceptance of the service does not replace the required approval.
66. How should I respond to a regulator's request?
Verify the scope and deadline, collect the correct documents and designate who will respond. Explain the facts accurately, including any shortcomings and remediation plan, without concealment or creating records that suggest earlier implementation that did not occur.
Keep a copy of the response and attachments and proof of submission, and check who is authorised to approve it. If the request is unclear, seek clarification through the official channel without assuming that this suspends the response deadline.

Legal Translation
67. What distinguishes a legal review of a translation?
It examines the effect of terminology and obligations, the governing language, and names and figures. A sentence may be linguistically correct yet change a right or condition in the other version. The texts must therefore be compared in the context of the full contract.
The review also covers dates, currencies, cross-references, annexes and whether the contract specifies a prevailing language version in case of conflict. Improving a translation must be clearly distinguished from amending the agreement itself, which may require the parties' consent.
68. Is machine translation suitable for official submission?
It is not, by itself, treated as a certified legal translation. Check the authority's requirements, document type and language, and use a registered translator where required. Automated tools may assist with an initial understanding if accompanied by appropriate human review.
Check confidentiality requirements before uploading a contract or personal file to any external tool. Even where the language is correct, the meaning of an obligation or exception may change if a sentence is not read in the context of the full document.
69. What is the difference between certified and ordinary translation?
A certified translation is produced under the relevant professional certification requirements and those of the receiving authority. An ordinary translation may serve understanding or internal business purposes. Translation certification differs from authentication of the original document or signature, and a transaction may require more than one procedure.
Ask the receiving authority exactly what it requires before work begins. It may require a translation it accepts, authentication of the original document, or both. Neither automatically substitutes for the other.
70. Which documents need legal translation?
It depends on their intended use: contracts, powers of attorney, judgments, company records, personal documents and others. Identify the authority and purpose first, then the language, certification and authentication required, rather than translating an entire file before knowing what will be used.
Send a complete, clear copy of the required document, including stamps, reverse sides and relevant annexes. Explain whether there is an earlier version or a difference in name spelling so that an existing error is not carried into the translation.

Business Services
71. What is the first step in setting up a company?
Define the actual activity, customers, operating location and ownership structure, then assess the licensing authority, legal form and approvals. Licence fees alone do not reflect the requirements for operations, employees, accounts and compliance.
Also plan post-incorporation costs, including renewals, premises, employees and regulatory requirements. The activity may require sector-specific approval in addition to the business licence.
72. Should I choose a free zone or the mainland?
Compare the options against the activity, market, customers, premises, approvals, budget and future plans. Also check tax and operational rules. Do not assume that one option is always better or tax-exempt.
Ask for a comparison tied to your business plan: where will services be provided, who will sign contracts, and where will the team and premises be based? The better choice may change with the operating model, even if the trade name stays the same.
73. Can the activity be changed after licensing?
An amendment can be requested under the authority's procedures and required approvals. Check the conditions before carrying out the new activity and its effect on contracts, taxes, compliance, premises and employees where relevant to the change.
Also check whether the change requires notifying authorities, partners or a bank, or updating other records. Submitting an amendment request does not mean that approvals are complete or every activity mentioned in it is authorised.
74. What should I prepare before a partner or investor joins?
The licence, constitutional documents, ownership register and details of authority, contracts and liabilities, financial and tax files, and rights in products or brands. Identify any promise of equity or unregistered rights so that due diligence shows the actual ownership and obligations.
Document the investment terms, valuation, contributions, information rights, exit arrangements and warranties. Then align the agreement with registration and equity-transfer procedures. Do not rely only on a marketing summary or oral promise of ownership.
