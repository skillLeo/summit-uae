Advice and Support in Criminal Cases in the UAE
When you receive a summons or learn of a criminal complaint, the first priority is to understand which authority is involved, the stage reached, the date set and what is required of you. We review the facts, evidence and chronology, identify urgent issues and define the legal assistance appropriate to the matter.
