Disclosure
Do I Need to Have an Existing Case?
No. You can request advice before signing a contract, accepting a settlement or entering a partnership, as well as to review an existing claim, case or judgment.
Do You Assist Both Individuals and Businesses?
Yes. We assist individuals, businesses, investors, entrepreneurs and residents with matters connected to the UAE. The engagement may cover a preventive review or work on an existing dispute.

Spine and method steps
1
Understand What Happened
We listen to the facts, put events in order, and identify the parties, what was agreed and what was carried out.
2
Review the Documents
We examine contracts, messages, transfers, judgments and other material relevant to the matter, and explain any gaps that need addressing.
3
Identify the Options
We assess the claim or defence and the available procedures, explaining the possible implications of each course of action.
4
Agree on the Next Step
We define the scope of service, fees, required documents and authorisations, and how the work will be followed up once the engagement is accepted.
