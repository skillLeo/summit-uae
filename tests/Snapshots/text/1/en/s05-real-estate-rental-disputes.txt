Property and Tenancy Disputes in Dubai and the UAE
A dispute may concern a delayed property handover, an undisclosed defect, a home that cannot be properly used or a deposit that has not been returned after move-out. We identify the obligation in dispute, how it can be proved and the remedy that addresses its consequences, while reviewing both parties’ obligations and the competent authority.
